About the National Helpline Against Atrocities (NHAA 14566)

The National Helpline Against Atrocities (NHAA - 14566) is an initiative by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India, created under the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and its 2016/2018 amendments.

Key Statutory Guarantees SC/ST (PoA) Act

Section 18A Mandate

No preliminary inquiry or prior permission required before registration of an FIR and prompt arrest.

Section 15A Protection

Statutory witness protection, state-sponsored travel allowance, and protection from economic boycotts.

Rule 12 Relief Schedule

Stage 1: 25% within 7 days. Stage 2: 50% on charge-sheet. Stage 3: 25% upon court trial conclusion.

60-Day Investigation

Statutory deadline for completion of investigation and charge-sheet submission by DSP Special Cell.

SC/ST (PoA) Act Statutory Rights Handbook

Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (1989 & 2016)

Official Government Handbook Bilingual Edition (English & हिन्दी)

1. Mandatory FIR Registration (Section 18A)

Under Section 18A introduced by the Parliament of India, no preliminary inquiry is permissible before registration of an FIR for offenses committed against members of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. The investigating officer has full authority to arrest accused persons without prior sanction.

2. Witness Protection Scheme (Section 15A)

The Act imposes a statutory duty on the State to ensure complete physical protection, safe transport, and maintenance allowance to victims and witnesses during investigation and court trials. Any intimidation or threat is punishable by up to 5 years imprisonment.

3. Strict 60-Day Investigation Deadline

Section 9 and Rule 7 mandate that the investigation must be conducted by an officer not below the rank of Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP) and completed within 60 days, followed by day-to-day trial completion by Exclusive Special Courts.

4. Monetary Relief & Rehabilitation (Rule 12)

Economic assistance is provided under Annexure-I ranging from ₹85,000 to ₹8,25,000 based on the schedule of offenses, with 25% payable immediately within 7 days of the FIR.
